Zenith Rider is an endless arcade game about a rider gliding down high snowy slopes. Jump off ramps, flip through the air, ride rope lines and time every landing.

Crystals are the only hazard on the slope. Crashing into one from the side ends the run, unless a shield, Zenith Flow, a dash or a smashing rider saves you. Landing on one from above shatters it and bounces you back into the air.

WHAT YOU DO
- Tap to jump. Jump from a launch ramp for a bigger leap.
- Hold in the air to flip backwards.
- Land a flip or a rope dismount on the snow and you get a one-second dash that speeds you up and shatters crystals ahead.
- Land on rope lines between posts to slide along them.
- Rising air currents lift you higher.
- Cross chasms from ramp to ramp, or use a rope to reach the far side.

COMBOS AND ZENITH FLOW
- Flips, rope rides and shattered crystals raise your combo. A higher combo gives you a longer scarf.
- At x5 with a full scarf, Zenith Flow ignites if you have unlocked it: you move faster, shatter crystals on contact and can open an energy wing to glide. Your combo resets when Flow ends.
- Zenith Flow is a one-time unlock in the shop.

SHOP
- Five upgrades with five levels each: Sphere Magnet, Zenith Wings, Flow Resonator, Zephyr Crest and Wisp Call.
- Modifiers to take into a run: Aegis Shield, Chasm Tether, Double Orbs, Lunar Gravity and Instant Zenith.
- Six riders with different speed, handling and abilities. Some can double jump, and some shatter crystals at speed.

MISSIONS
- Three missions are active at a time, and progress stays ticked between runs. Finish all three to rank up and receive a new set.

WORLD
- The day cycle runs from dawn through day, golden hour, sunset, dusk and night. Each run starts at a different hour. Lanterns glow at dusk; at night you can see stars, a crescent moon and the northern lights.
- Weather changes on its own: clear, cloudy, snow, blizzard, fog, rain and thunderstorms with lightning. Weather changes the look and the sound, not the rules.
- Landmarks, groves and lantern posts are placed anew in every run.

SOUND
- All music and sound effects are generated in real time by the game. The ambient music follows your height, the time of day and Zenith Flow.

LANGUAGES
- English and Ukrainian. Switch between them in the menu.

SCORING
- Your score adds up distance, trick points multiplied by your combo, energy orbs, chasms crossed and completed missions.
